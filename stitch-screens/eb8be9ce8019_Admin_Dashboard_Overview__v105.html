Why MFA Matters?

MFA blocks 99.9% of account takeover attacks. By enabling it, you aren't just protecting yourself, but the entire fleet's data integrity.

lightbulb MFA FAQ Snippets

Common Question
"What if I lose my phone?"

Encourage users to generate recovery codes or set up a secondary method (like a hardware key) during the initial onboarding flow.

Pro-Tip for Admins
Hardware Key Incentive

Providing free hardware keys can increase MFA adoption by 15% in high-risk departments like Finance or HR.

Policy Highlight
Conditional Access

Explain to employees that MFA is only triggered during suspicious logins, not every single time, to reduce friction.
